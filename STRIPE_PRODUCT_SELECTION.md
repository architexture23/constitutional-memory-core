# Stripe Product Selection Guide

**Built from Remembrance | Operating under Format Law**

## Which Stripe Product to Choose?

### ✅ **Choose: "Non-recurring payments"**

**Why:**
- Truth Drop Platform sells **individual codexes** (one-time purchases)
- Each codex has a **single price** (e.g., $99.99, $49.99)
- Buyers purchase **once** and own the codex permanently
- Not subscriptions (no recurring billing)
- Not a marketplace (you're the only seller)

### Your Business Model:
- ✅ **Product Sales:** Individual codexes
- ✅ **One-time Payment:** Each codex purchased separately
- ✅ **Permanent Access:** Buyers keep access forever
- ❌ **No Subscriptions:** Not monthly/yearly fees
- ❌ **Not a Marketplace:** Just you selling (not multiple sellers)

### What "Non-recurring payments" Includes:
1. **Checkout Page** - Stripe's hosted checkout (recommended)
2. **Payment Links** - Send payment requests via email
3. **Invoices** - Send professional invoices
4. **One-time Payments** - Perfect for individual product sales

### What You Can Do With This:
- ✅ Create checkout for each codex purchase
- ✅ Accept credit cards, debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay
- ✅ Get paid automatically to your bank
- ✅ Track all sales in Stripe dashboard
- ✅ Issue refunds if needed
- ✅ Generate invoices/receipts automatically

## What About the Other Options?

### "Recurring payments" (Don't Choose This)
- For subscriptions (monthly/yearly fees)
- Not needed for one-time codex purchases
- You could add this later if you want subscription plans

### "Build a platform or marketplace" (Don't Choose This)
- For multi-seller marketplaces (like Etsy, Uber)
- Allows money movement between multiple parties
- Not needed if you're the only seller

**You can always add other products later if your business model changes.**

## Next Steps After Selection

1. **Click "Non-recurring payments"**
2. **Get Started** with checkout
3. **Complete business information:**
   - Business Name: "Truth Drop Platform"
   - Website: Skip or use localhost
   - Bank account (for payouts)
4. **Get API Keys:**
   - Test Mode keys (for development)
   - Live Mode keys (for production, later)

## Recommended Setup

**Start with:**
- ✅ Non-recurring payments
- ✅ Test Mode (for development)
- ✅ Stripe Checkout (easiest to implement)

**Later (Optional):**
- Add recurring payments if you want subscription plans
- Stay with non-recurring for individual sales

## Summary

This is perfect for:
- Individual codex sales

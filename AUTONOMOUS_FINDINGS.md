5. Copy the token (starts with `rw_...`)

### Vercel API Token

**Location I Found:**
- URL: `https://vercel.com/account/settings/tokens`
- Requires: Login (GitHub, Google, Email, or SAML)

**How to Access:**
1. Navigate to: `https://vercel.com/login?next=%2Faccount%2Fsettings%2Ftokens`
2. Login with your method (GitHub, Google, Email, etc.)
3. After login, you'll be redirected to tokens page
4. Click "Create Token" button
5. Copy the token (starts with `vercel_...`)

**Alternative Path:**
- Go to vercel.com
- Click your profile icon (top right)
- Select "Settings"
- Click "Tokens" in sidebar
- Click "Create Token"

## Why These Features?

### Tax Collection:
- **Automatic:** Stripe calculates tax based on customer location
- **Compliance:** Required in many places (US states, EU, etc.)
- **Easy:** No manual tax calculation needed
- **Important:** Helps avoid legal issues

### Fraud Protection:
- **Automatic:** Stripe detects fraudulent cards
- **Protection:** Reduces chargebacks and disputes
- **Smart:** Uses machine learning to detect fraud
- **Free:** Included with Stripe
